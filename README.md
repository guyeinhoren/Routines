# Overview
Routines helps you build and stick to the habits that matter to you, from morning exercise to reading before bed, while tracking your progress visually.
Each routine gets a square for every day of the week that fills in gradually as you go, so you can see your whole week at a glance and know exactly where you stand.

## FEATURES

A weekly log you can see and feel Every routine has seven squares, one for each day, that fill in with every repetition you log. Set how many times a day you want to do each routine, and add a full-screen timer for routines that need set time, like meditation or stretching.
Organize with lists 

Group your routines into lists, such as "Morning Routine" or "Workout," and give each list its own icon and color. Pin the lists you use most to the home screen as tiles, alongside an "All" view that brings all your routines together in one place.
Workouts and history tracking 

One tap starts a workout that takes you through every routine in a list, one after another. When the workout ends, you can automatically add an event to your calendar at the exact time it took place. Each routine also has a monthly history calendar showing how much you did, day by day, and each list displays a heat map that shows how consistent you've been over time.
Made for iPhone and Mac 

Routines is built from the ground up for iPhone and Mac and feels right at home on both. Your data syncs across your devices with iCloud, so your routines and their history are always up to date wherever you open the app. The app is available in Hebrew and English, with full right-to-left support on every screen.
Privacy first 

Routines has no ads, requires no sign-up, and doesn't track you. All your information, including routine names, photos, and history, is stored only in your private iCloud and is never sent to any external server.
